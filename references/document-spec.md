# Document and upload specification

## Output folders

Create:

```text
case-packet/
├── 00-材料准备情况及待确认事项.docx
├── 00-材料准备情况及待确认事项.pdf
├── 01-民事起诉状.docx OR 01-报案事实说明.docx
├── 01-民事起诉状.pdf  OR 01-报案事实说明.pdf
├── 02-证据目录.docx
├── 02-证据目录.pdf
├── 03-证据材料.pdf
└── 04-人民法院在线服务上传清单.docx/pdf  (civil route only)
```

Draft filenames and first-page titles must contain `草稿-待核对`. Remove that label only after the final gate passes.

## Civil complaint content

Use the applicable 2025 Supreme People's Court element-style template when available. For an online goods transaction, use the sale-contract template structure, including:

- natural-person/entity party information;
- concrete requests and total amount;
- jurisdiction/arbitration and pre-suit preservation fields;
- contract formation and parties;
- goods, price, payment, delivery, inspection, quality, performance, reminders, refund/termination, breach/loss, guarantee, and legal/contract basis as applicable;
- evidence list;
- mediation preference;
- user signature/seal line and date.

Do not fill irrelevant fields with invented text. Use `无`, leave blank, or mark `待确认` in a draft as the official form permits.

## Police-report statement content

- reporting person and contact;
- factual chronology;
- transaction and payment details;
- counterpart accounts and identity clues;
- representations made and later conduct;
- loss calculation;
- platform/payment complaints and responses;
- evidence list;
- requested review phrased without declaring guilt;
- signature and date line.

## Evidence index columns

Use: `序号 | 证据名称 | 来源及形成时间 | 对应文件/页码 | 证明对象和内容 | 原始载体 | 完整性说明`.

If the table becomes unreadable, use landscape only for the evidence index or split long detail into labeled paragraphs; never shrink below readable text.

## Document style

Use a restrained formal Chinese legal-document style:

- A4 portrait; 25 mm left/right, 25 mm top/bottom unless an official template controls layout;
- Songti/SimSun body with compatible CJK fallback; 11-12 pt;
- centered bold title; black text; no decorative colors;
- 1.5 line spacing for narrative where practical;
- explicit table widths and repeating header rows;
- footer page numbers on multi-page documents;
- no signatures, seals, or handwritten marks generated by the system.

This is a named `chinese_court_a4` override of the document system's `standard_business_brief` preset: A4 replaces Letter, Chinese legal typography replaces Calibri, and all colors remain black/gray.

For macOS LibreOffice visual QA, set `FONTCONFIG_FILE=assets/fontconfig-macos.conf` when the isolated renderer cannot discover system Chinese fonts. This affects QA rendering only and does not alter the evidence or pleading content.

## Live upload check

Before finalization, ask the user for a screenshot of the live upload screen only if current file type, size, count, or category requirements are not otherwise visible. Extract those requirements from the screenshot; do not ask the user to retype them.
