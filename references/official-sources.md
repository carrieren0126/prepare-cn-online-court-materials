## Verification protocol

1. Search the exact document title on an official domain.
2. Confirm issuing body, document number, effective date, and amendment/repeal status.
3. Prefer the National People's Congress, State Council, Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Justice, or a government/court-system host.
4. Record the URL and date checked in the case workspace.
5. If official sources conflict or a later source is unclear, stop finalization and route to human review.
