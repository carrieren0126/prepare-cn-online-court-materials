# Electronic-evidence handling

## Minimum evidence groups

1. Identity: plaintiff identity; counterparty or account-identification clues.
2. Transaction formation: listing, offer, order, contract, chat agreement, platform rules where relevant.
3. Payment: official bill, transfer detail, receiving account, order number, time, amount, and channel.
4. Performance or breach: delivery/logistics, service result, non-delivery, discrepancy, defect, failed withdrawal, or refusal.
5. Reminder and response: full chat context, calls/messages, refund demand, admissions, or blocking/deletion.
6. Loss and remedy: refund status, additional expense, replacement cost, or other claimed amount with calculation support.
7. Platform/authority records: complaint receipt, customer-service response, payment dispute, report receipt, or mediation record.

## Collection and preservation

- Preserve the original app, device, file, export, and downloadable platform/payment record where available.
- Capture the account profile and identifier before the content; then capture the complete conversation or transaction sequence.
- Keep timestamps, counterpart identity, message continuity, order number, and payment-account details visible.
- Avoid isolated cropped screenshots when fuller context is available.
- For long or dynamic content, preserve both screenshots and a continuous screen recording where lawful and practical.
- Do not annotate, compress, filter, rename destructively, or overwrite the only original. Work on a labeled copy.
- Record who collected the evidence, when, from which device/account, and how it was exported.
- Prefer platform-generated bills or records over manually typed summaries.
- Treat notarization, trusted timestamping, or blockchain storage as possible supporting methods, not automatic proof that underlying content is true.

## Evidence index fields

For every item include:

- sequence number;
- evidence name;
- category;
- source and formation/collection time;
- concise object and content to prove;
- corresponding file and page/message range;
- original carrier or custody location;
- integrity/completeness note;
- whether an original or platform record can be produced.

The submitter must sign or seal and state the submission date. Do not generate a signature or seal.

## Packaging rules

- Use one evidence number for one coherent item or tightly related group.
- Put a cover page before each numbered item.
- Ensure the number and name match the evidence index exactly.
- Add continuous packet page numbers after assembly when tooling permits; otherwise include reliable file/page pointers in the index.
- Keep personal-data redaction reversible and label the redacted copy. Court or police versions may require unredacted data.
